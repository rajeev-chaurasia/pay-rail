# Non-goals

This project does not supply a bank, payment processor, card vault, compliance
certification or real money movement. The provider exists to exercise stable
operation keys, acknowledgement ambiguity and recovery.

It does not infer performance from a small local run, count rejected requests
as accepted mutations, or substitute repeated requests for concurrency evidence.
Scale claims require the separately sized qualification described in the plan.

Contributor alignment does not copy unrelated clinical workflows, staff roles,
language-specific tools or other projects' product promises into PayRail.
